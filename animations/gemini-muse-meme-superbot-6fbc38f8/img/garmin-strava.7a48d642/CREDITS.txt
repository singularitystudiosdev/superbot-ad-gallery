map.svg     The Strava card's map: a 560 x 200 SVG drawn from OpenStreetMap data, styled after Strava's light map (warm grey
            land, green parks, blue water, white streets on a grey casing, primaries pale yellow, trails dashed). No map
            tile, embed or API key is loaded. Streets (highway motorway..residential, plus footway / path / cycleway over
            90 m that are not sidewalks or crossings), parks, pitches, grass, water, streams, the CSU campus and rail,
            fetched with one Overpass query (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter, `out geom`) over the box
            40.5615,-105.1385,40.5965,-105.0445, then projected (equirectangular at the route's mid latitude) and
            simplified (Douglas-Peucker, 0.3 to 0.4 px).

            The attribution sits on the map itself, bottom right, as Strava's own maps carry theirs.
            Fetched 2026-10-17.

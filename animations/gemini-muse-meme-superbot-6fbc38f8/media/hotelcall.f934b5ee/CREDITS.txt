call.mp3
  The phone call superbot makes to the hotel, 21.79 s, 48 kHz mono, 128 kbps MP3, -16.4 LUFS integrated. Assembled by
  script from four GENERATED voice lines, 2026-10-17. The hotel, "The Calder", its front-desk agent "Maya", the guest
  "Sam Rivera" and the call itself are fictional: no hotel was called and no reservation exists.
  Voices: Gemini TTS, gemini-3.8-flash-tts (Cosmos media speak, provider google, en-US), one call per line:
    Maya      voice sulafat  "The Calder, this is Maya. How can I help?"
    Superbot  voice achird   "Hi Maya, I'm Superbot, Sam Rivera's AI assistant. Could we move Sam's stay to the 25th
                              through the 30th, instead of the 20th to the 25th?"
    Maya      voice sulafat  "Sure, one sec. Okay, same king room, same rate. You're all set for the 25th through the 30th."
    Superbot  voice achird   "Perfect, thanks so much!"
  (A set from OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts, voices coral and cedar, was made too and dropped: a listening pass by Gemini 3.8
  Flash rated it 4/10 against this set's 9 and 8.5.)
  Edits: leading and trailing silence trimmed; Maya's pause after "one sec." cut from 1.20 s to 0.75 s; both voices
  time-stretched 1.06x (ffmpeg atempo); Maya band-limited to a phone line (two 12 dB/oct highpasses at 320 Hz, two
  lowpasses at 3.3 kHz) and compressed 3:1, Superbot left full band, compressed 2:1; Maya +0.5 dB and Superbot -1.5 dB
  (a listening pass heard the full-band side about 2 dB hotter). Laid end to end with 0.30, 0.28 and 0.25 s turn gaps,
  under one synthesized US ringback ring (440 + 480 Hz, 1.55 s), a pickup click, a 300 Hz to 2.8 kHz line hiss near
  -50 dBFS and a hang-up click; then two-pass linear loudnorm (one static gain, so the hiss never pumps) to -16 LUFS,
  -1.5 dBTP, which the true-peak ceiling holds at -16.4.
  Word timings in ../../scenes/tabs-assets/beats/hotelcall-data.f934b5ee.js: whisper.cpp 1.9.5, model ggml-base.en with
  DTW token timestamps, run on each clean line; Maya's second line re-aligned phrase by phrase between its pauses (the
  whole-line pass drifted up to 0.5 s on her long "Okay,").
  The build script lives outside the repo (Quick chats .tmp/hotel/tools/build_call.py, 2026-10-17).

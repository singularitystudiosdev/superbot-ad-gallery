artist ("Velvet Static", "Afterglow Tour"), the date, the listings and every price are invented for the spot, so no
real performer is shown and no price claims to be live. Ball Arena, Denver is a real venue; its seat map here is a
generic arena drawn in SVG, not the venue's real chart.

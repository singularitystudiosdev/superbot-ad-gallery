Sources for the ROB IT thumbnail spot (screen.d35be136.js, chat.d35be136.js). Never shown on screen.

All page captures are new, taken 2026-10-21 for this spot: 1728x1035 CSS (the page area of full-screen Chrome on a
1728x1117 desktop, a 16-inch MacBook Pro's default) at 2x, so each file is 3456x2070. Nothing on any page was changed
before capture.

game-c0..game-c4.webp: https://www.roblox.com/games/96168869671905/ROB-IT, the public game page, captured logged out in a
  headless Chromium with a throwaway profile (no account, no personal details), the pointer resting over the carousel
  so its arrows show. c0 is the carousel on the game's trailer video, c1..c4 the same page after one to four clicks of
  its Next arrow: the game's four public thumbnails in their carousel order.

hub-view.webp, hub-edit.webp, hub-col.webp:
  https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations/experiences/9472441390/places/96168869671905/thumbnails, ROB IT's
  Creator Hub Thumbnails page, captured in the owner's own signed-in Chrome through Cosmos browser-control, read only.
  hub-view: the page as it opens. hub-edit: the page after "Edit active thumbnails", content column at the top.
  hub-col: that content column stitched from three captures of the same view at scroll 0, 900 and 1170.5 (its full
  2134px, the window's overlay scrollbar left out); the animation scrolls this under the page's fixed header and
  rails, and draws the scrollbar's thumb at the size and colour measured from the capture. Nothing was uploaded,
  checked, unchecked or saved; the edit view's local preference was set back so the page opens in view mode again.
  Every figure is the one Creator Hub showed at 2:00 AM data on Oct 21, published as captured at the owner's choice.

cb-on.png, cb-off.png, row-blank.webp, rows-inactive.webp: crops of hub-col at 2x: row 2's checked box, row 6's
  unchecked box, row 9 (an unchecked upload with no data) and rows 6 to 10 (the unchecked uploads). superbot's
  unchecking and checking are these real pixels; a new take is shown as row 9's blank row with the take on its
  thumbnail, landing on row 6 with the unchecked rows moving down one. Where Creator Hub really places a new upload
  in its paginated table (10 rows a page, 19 thumbnails) was not tested: nothing was uploaded to the real game.

take1.webp, take2.webp, take3.webp: made for this spot with Nano Banana 2.1 (google/nano-banana-2.1/edit on fal,
  2026-10-21), prompt exactly "Make a novel perspective of this thumbnail", one of ROB IT's public thumbnails attached
  as the reference (https://thumbnails.roblox.com/v1/games/multiget/thumbnails?universeIds=9472441390, 768x432), at
  16:9, 1K. The reference for each round was picked at random (Math.random, recorded): take1 and take2 from thumbnail
  4 (the sleeping owner and the diamond), take3 from thumbnail 2 (the vault diamond). A fourth take was generated and
  not used.
